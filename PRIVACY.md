# Hisaab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: **October 2026**

Hisaab ("the app") is a personal finance and group expense tracker. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

## 1. Your financial data stays on your device
All data you enter — accounts, expenses, incomes, wallets, group balances, loans and payments — is stored **locally on your device**. The developer does **not** collect, transmit, or store your financial records on any server.

## 2. Google Drive backup (optional)
If you choose to sign in with Google and enable backup, your backup file is uploaded **only to your own Google Drive**, in a folder called *HisaabBackup*.

- The developer **cannot see, open, or restore anyone's backup** — ever.
- The app's permission is limited to files it created itself (`drive.file` scope).
- You can delete your backups at any time from drive.google.com, or disconnect in the app (Settings → Backup & restore → Sign out of Google).

## 3. Google Sign-In
Signing in is optional (the app works fully without it). When used, only your Google account email and display name are read — for linking your backup. Authentication is handled by Google Firebase.

## 4. Advertising
The app displays advertisements served by **Unity Ads** (and **Google AdMob** where enabled). These providers may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage information to serve and measure ads, under their own policies. **No financial or personal data from your records is shared with ad networks.** You may limit ad personalisation in your device Settings → Google → Ads.

## 5. In-app updates
The app checks a public version file (version.json) hosted on GitHub Pages to know when an update is available. No personal data is sent.

## 6. Backups you create manually
If you use Export Backup, the file is created and stored by you — you are responsible for where you save or send it.

## 7. Permissions
Internet permission is used solely for advertisements, update checks, and Google Drive backup (when you enable it). The app does not access your contacts, photos, messages, or other files.

## 8. Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

## 9. Your rights
Since your data never leaves your device (except to your own Drive if you opt in), you may erase everything at any time: delete backups from your Drive and use the app's clear/uninstall options.
